Client Journey

See the booking experience
through your client's eyes

From discovering the studio to managing bookings from a personal dashboard — every step of the client journey is designed to feel effortless and on-brand.

Try the App Studio Operations →
Step 01

Discover the studio

Clients arrive at a clean, welcoming public home page that showcases the studio's brand, available classes, and upcoming schedule. A strong first impression sets the tone for the whole relationship.

  • Studio branding and class overview on the landing page
  • Direct path from discovery straight into booking
  • Mobile-friendly layout that works on any device
Step 02

Browse classes

A well-organized class browser lets clients explore everything on offer at a glance. Each listing surfaces the details they need to make a decision quickly, without digging through menus.

  • Browse by class type, schedule, and instructor
  • Class cards show duration, capacity, and open spots
  • Consistent layout across all class listings
Step 03

Select a class or session

Clients view full class details and choose a specific session time. Clear availability indicators remove the guesswork — clients always know exactly how many spots remain before committing.

  • Full class detail view with instructor and session info
  • Real-time availability and remaining spot count
  • One-tap session selection to enter the booking flow
Step 04

Sign in or create an account

New clients create an account in seconds. Returning clients sign in and pick up exactly where they left off. The authentication flow is secure, minimal, and stays out of the way of the booking.

  • Account creation with email verification
  • Returning client sign-in with session persistence
  • Secure, lightweight authentication that doesn't interrupt the flow
Step 05

Choose a payment option

Clients with active memberships or class packs can apply their entitlement directly. Others can purchase a new plan on the spot or pay per session. Every client type finds a natural path forward.

  • Apply an existing membership or class pack
  • Purchase a new plan directly in the flow
  • Pay per session via Stripe checkout
Step 06

Confirm booking or complete checkout

Bookings are confirmed with a clear summary before any payment is processed. For paid sessions, Stripe handles checkout securely with a clean, trusted interface clients already recognize.

  • Booking summary shown before confirmation
  • Stripe-powered secure checkout for paid sessions
  • Clear confirmation screen with full booking details
Step 07

Manage bookings from the dashboard

After booking, clients have a personal dashboard to manage everything in one place. Upcoming sessions, booking history, membership status, and account details are all accessible at any time.

  • Upcoming and past bookings at a glance
  • Membership and plan status
  • Profile and account management
