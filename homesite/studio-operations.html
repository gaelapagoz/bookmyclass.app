Studio Operations

The operating system behind the studio

BookMyClass gives studio staff one place to manage schedules, customers, attendance, memberships, payments, communications, and day-to-day exceptions.

Operational context

Boutique studios run with limited administrative capacity and many moving parts

Recurring schedules, multiple access models, capacity constraints, customer exceptions, instructor coordination, payment state, and communication state all affect the day. The software needs to support routine work and the exceptions that interrupt it.

Operational responsibilities Schedules, sessions, capacity, customers, entitlements, attendance, orders, payments, email automation, support inbox, and role boundaries.

Responsibility areas

How studio work is organized in the product

Schedule and capacity

Classes, sessions, calendar work, and capacity signals stay visible

Admin users manage the class catalogue and sessions, including timing, teacher assignment where active, booking counts, capacity, and active waitlist counts for operational visibility.

Customer and attendance operations

Rosters, customer profiles, and attendance are handled from admin and session surfaces

Supported operations include customer profiles, booking history, attendee rosters, manual attendee add/remove with server-side eligibility validation, client self check-in, and staff attendance finalization.

Memberships and commercial products

Product + Price is the catalogue source of truth

The admin product and pricing surfaces support memberships, class packs, subscriptions, active or inactive availability, pricing, and customer entitlements or ownership using the current Product + Price model.

Payments and orders

Stripe checkout is reconciled back into local operational state

Confirmed flows include Stripe checkout, order and payment status, success reconciliation, idempotent webhook handling, purchase history, subscription ownership, and product-level ownership records that staff can reason about.

Communication and support

Email flows and support messages are treated as operational work

Supported surfaces include booking communications, cancellations, waitlist communication, account-security email flows, configurable email automations, admin inbox review, support/contact messages, and delivery outcomes where the current product exposes them.

Operational visibility

Admin screens make current activity easier to inspect

The current product provides visibility into booking activity, attendance, memberships, payment or order status, admin workload, communication state, analytics surfaces, and dashboard summaries where implemented. Planned diagnostics are not presented here as delivered capability.

Exception handling

Operational software has to handle non-happy paths

Class reaches capacity

Capacity and waitlist behavior protect overbooking for supported collective classes.

Client lacks access

Booking and manual add flows require eligible credit, qualifying subscription, or payment.

Cancellation occurs

Cancellation policy controls booking changes and credit restoration where supported.

Client joins waitlist

Join/cancel and eligibility-aware promotion are active for collective classes.

Payment needs reconciliation

Webhook and success-page reconciliation update order, payment, booking, and entitlement state.

Admin manually adds attendee

Server-side checks still enforce eligibility before the attendee is added.

Email outcome changes

Marketing and automation delivery failures are represented as explicit failed outcomes.

Account is unverified

Protected customer routes require verified account status.

Roles and boundaries

Current roles are explicit, with server-side access checks

Client

Books classes, manages dashboard, membership, wallet, profile, security, orders, cancellation, waitlist, and supported attendance interactions.

Teacher

Teacher routes and own-session roster management exist, but teacher capability is narrower than admin capability.

Admin

Accesses the private admin area for operational work through server-side admin session guards and service-backed actions.

Master Admin

Privileged role assignment and management is protected server-side; ordinary admin access does not imply full role-management power.
