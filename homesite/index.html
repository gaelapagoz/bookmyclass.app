Project context

From studio operations to a working software product

BookMyClass started from the operating needs of a boutique studio in Brussels offering yoga, Pilates, fitness, private coaching, and company classes. The work covered the client booking experience and the administrative workflows required to manage sessions, access, payments, attendance, and communication.

Operating needs translated into product rules
  • Class discovery, capacity, and session booking.
  • Memberships, class packs, subscriptions, and credits.
  • Payments, reconciliation, cancellations, and waitlists.
  • Customer communication, support, and staff operations.

Product scope

Implemented domains

Accounts

Client registration, sign-in, verification, password reset, profile, and account security flows.

Booking

Class discovery, session selection, booking drafts, cancellation policy, waitlists, and attendance.

Commerce

Memberships, class packs, subscriptions, products, gift cards, coupons, orders, and Stripe checkout.

Operations

Admin and teacher surfaces for sessions, customers, rosters, products, learning content, inbox, and automations.

Selected workflows

Workflows designed around studio decisions

Client booking

A client chooses a class, signs in, applies eligible access or starts payment, and receives a confirmed booking when the rules are satisfied.

Studio operations

Staff inspect schedules, capacity, attendees, customers, memberships, orders, communication state, and exceptions from administrative surfaces.

Payment completion

Stripe Checkout collects payment. Webhooks and success-page reconciliation update local booking, order, payment, and entitlement state.

Engineering summary

Engineering behind the product

Product architecture

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL support a modular monolith with business rules grouped in service modules.

Payments and workflows

Authentication, bookings, entitlement validation, subscriptions, Stripe checkout, reconciliation, attendance, waitlists, and administration are connected.

Reliability and operations

Nginx, PM2, Docker-managed PostgreSQL, Sentry, uptime monitoring, scheduled maintenance, backups, and controlled release promotion support operation.

Quality and delivery

Unit, integration, UI, end-to-end, performance, security, and manual release validation are used according to change risk.

Boundaries

The public demo is a single-studio demonstration platform with sample data. Multi-tenant architecture remains a future evolution, not an active claim.

Current status and limitations

Public demo with transparent boundaries

BookMyClass is a privately developed studio-management platform with a public demonstration environment. It demonstrates implemented workflows and engineering decisions without implying external studio rollout, business results, or multi-studio scale.

Demo status

  • Sample data may be reset.
  • Payment behavior is demonstration or test behavior.
  • No real classes are booked.
  • Outbound email may be suppressed or controlled.
