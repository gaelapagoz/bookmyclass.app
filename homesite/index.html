FAQ

Common questions from studio owners

Can clients book classes online?

Yes. Clients can browse class availability and reserve sessions online through a simple booking experience.

Can I sell memberships and packs?

Yes. You can offer memberships and class packs so clients can choose the format that fits their routine.

Does it include payments?

Yes. Stripe powers checkout, giving your studio a secure and familiar payment flow.

Can customers manage their own accounts?

Yes. Customers can manage bookings and profile information from their self-service dashboard.

Does it include an admin dashboard?

Yes. The admin back office centralizes classes, users, products, subscriptions, content, and inbox operations.

Is it suitable for boutique studios?

Yes. BookMyClass is tailored for boutique fitness teams that need premium customer experience and structured operations.
